THE CITY IS CALLING.

A fan experience that moves with Houston: scan, earn, unlock, repeat. Younger fans get play, progress and real-world events. Longtime fans keep the game-day app they already know.

Attract younger fans

  • Scan QR codes at event stations for points and climb 5 tiers, Rookie to Hall of Fame
  • Your rocket climbs with your points and gets a new upgrade with each win
  • Fantasy: build a $20 starting 5 from every era of Rockets history, plus game predictions
  • Draft War Room: vote on Houston’s pick in the offseason and earn points if you call it
  • Challenges, badges and a Houston leaderboard
  • Youth & college events: AI literacy, 3v3, H.O.R.S.E., skills challenges, pop-ups

Keep longtime fans

  • Classic view: one toggle hides the game layer, leaving tickets, events and shop
  • Losses still earn points, so loyalty is rewarded, not just winning streaks
  • Legacy Wall and Fan Family link longtime fans with new ones
